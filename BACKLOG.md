# Backlog

Feature requests and planned extensions. Entries here will **not** be implemented until explicitly prioritized and moved into a release branch.

Not everything that is missing is planned. Where a request is declined on principle rather than on
priority, the reason is written down once in [what Yuvomi will not become](docs/SCOPE.md). The
direction the open threads add up to, without dates, is in [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md).

New suggestion? → [Start a discussion](https://github.com/ulsklyc/yuvomi/discussions/new?category=ideas). Bugs go straight to issues; an idea becomes an issue once it has been cut to shape.
